EcoRouter

Use less AI. Get the answer you need.

EcoRouter is a connected AI workspace designed to make artificial intelligence more efficient, collaborative, accessible, and environmentally responsible.

Developed by ChatGPTree, Inc. in partnership with Compassion Ventures.

Website: https://ecorouter.io

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

EcoRouter Chat — Intelligent multi-provider AI routing, reusable knowledge, and search-grounded responses.

Chalkboard — AI-powered visual brainstorming, digital whiteboards, and creative collaboration.

Canopy — Connected team communication, organizational collaboration, and knowledge sharing. Currently in private beta.

Reply Studio — Intelligent communication and context-aware response drafting.

EcoAgent — AI-powered productivity and workflow assistance.

Workspaces & Groves — Structured personal and organizational knowledge environments.

EcoReceipt — Environmental and economic transparency for AI usage, including estimated energy consumption, carbon emissions, and computational costs.

Developer Infrastructure — API access, embeddable AI capabilities, and enterprise integration opportunities.
